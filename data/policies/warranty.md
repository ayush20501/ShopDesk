# Warranty

## Warranty periods
Warranty starts on the delivery date.

- Electronics (earphones, smartwatches, speakers): 12 months
- Appliances (fans, coffee makers): 24 months
- Accessories (cables, chargers, cases, mice, stands): 6 months
- Power banks and batteries: 6 months
- Home items (lamps, blankets, storage boxes, yoga mats): no warranty, only the 30 day return window

## What is covered
Manufacturing defects and faults that appear during normal use.

## What is not covered
Water or liquid damage, drops and physical damage, burn marks from wrong voltage, and repairs done by someone other than us.

## How to claim
Open a support ticket with your order number and a photo or short video of the problem. We repair the item within 7 business days. If it cannot be repaired, we replace it.

Defects found within 48 hours of delivery are handled as returns, not warranty claims.
