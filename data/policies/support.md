# Support

## Support hours
Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, from 9 AM to 9 PM. The chat assistant works all day.

## What the chat assistant can do
It answers questions about our policies, looks up your own orders, refunds and tickets, and can prepare cancellations, returns and support tickets. Returns, refunds and cancellations are always confirmed by a support manager before they happen.

## Privacy
The assistant only discusses the orders and details of the account you are signed in to. It cannot share anyone else's information.

## Escalation
If the assistant cannot answer, it opens a support ticket. A person replies within one business day.
