# Returns

## Return windows
You can return most items within a fixed number of days after delivery. The count starts on the delivery date shown on your order, and the last day is included.

- Electronics (earphones, smartwatches, speakers, power banks): 10 days
- Appliances (fans, coffee makers): 10 days
- Accessories (cables, chargers, phone cases, mice, laptop stands): 15 days
- Home items (lamps, blankets, storage boxes, yoga mats): 30 days

If an order has items from different categories, each item follows its own window.

## Items that cannot be returned
In-ear earphones cannot be returned for change of mind once the seal is broken, because of hygiene rules. They can still be returned if they are defective or damaged.

Items that are used, washed, or missing parts or original packaging are not accepted.

## Defective or damaged items
If an item arrives defective or damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery. We will replace it or refund it in full, and pickup is free. Reports made after 48 hours are handled as warranty claims instead of returns (see the warranty policy).

## How to return
Raise a return from the chat or the Orders page. Pack the item in its original packaging. Our courier picks it up within 2 to 3 business days.

For change-of-mind returns, a pickup fee of ₹99 is deducted from the refund. Pickup is free for defective or damaged items.
