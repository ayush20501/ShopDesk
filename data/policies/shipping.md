# Shipping

## Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free when the order value is above ₹999. For orders of ₹999 or less, shipping costs ₹79. The order value is the price of the items, before any fees.

## Delivery speeds
- Standard: 4-6 business days to any serviceable pincode in India.
- Express: 1-2 business days, ₹149, available only in metro cities.

Metro cities for express delivery: Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Pune.

Gold tier members get express delivery for free on every order. Silver tier members get one free express delivery per month.

## Where we deliver
We deliver only within India. We do not ship to other countries.

## Tracking and delivery attempts
You get tracking details by SMS and email when the order is shipped. The courier makes 3 delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt the parcel goes back to our warehouse and the order is refunded.

## Changing the address
The delivery address can be changed only while the order status is placed, before it is shipped.
