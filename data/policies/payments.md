# Payments

## Payment methods
We accept credit and debit cards, UPI, net banking, wallets and cash on delivery.

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹5000 and has a fee of ₹49. Orders above ₹5000 must be paid online.

## EMI
EMI is available on credit cards for orders of ₹3000 and above, for 3 or 6 months. Orders below ₹3000 are not eligible for EMI. On orders of ₹10000 and above, 3 month EMI has no extra interest.

## Failed payments
If money was deducted but the order was not placed, the amount is refunded automatically within 5-7 business days. You do not need to contact us unless it takes longer.

## Invoices
A GST invoice is emailed when the order is shipped and can be downloaded from the Orders page.
