# Refunds

## When refunds start
After the courier picks up a returned item, it is inspected at our warehouse within 2 business days. The refund is started once the item passes inspection.

## Refund timelines
How long the money takes to reach you depends on how you paid. These times count from the day the refund is started.

- UPI: 1-2 business days
- Credit and debit cards: 5-7 business days
- Net banking: 3-5 business days
- Wallets: within 24 hours
- Cash on delivery orders: bank transfer within 7 business days, after you share your bank details with support
- Store credit: instant

## Store credit bonus
If you choose store credit instead of your original payment method, you get 5% extra credit. Store credit does not expire for 12 months.

## Partial refunds
If only some items in an order are returned, only those items are refunded. Shipping fees and cash-on-delivery fees are not refunded unless the whole order is cancelled or the item was defective.

## Refund approval
Every refund is checked and approved by a support manager before money is sent.
