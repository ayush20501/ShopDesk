# Loyalty program

## Earning points
You earn 1 point for every ₹100 spent on items. Shipping and cash-on-delivery fees do not earn points. Points are added 7 days after delivery.

## Using points
100 points are worth ₹50. Points can pay for up to 20% of an order's value.

## Expiry
Points expire 12 months after the day they were earned.

## Tiers
- Basic: below 500 points
- Silver: 500 points and above. One free express delivery per month.
- Gold: 2000 points and above. Free express delivery on every order and early access to sales.

Your tier is based on your current points balance.
